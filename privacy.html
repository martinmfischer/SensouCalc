Privacy & local data

Sensou Calc calculates dice probabilities on your device. It does not require a user account.

Scenarios and offline use

Your current settings and saved scenarios are stored in this browser’s local storage. The offline app uses a service worker and browser cache for its own files. These features do not send your scenarios to a database. Storage is specific to the site address and browser; it does not automatically sync between devices.

You can delete individual saved scenarios in the Scenarios dialog. Reset restores the current inputs but keeps saved scenarios. To remove all locally stored app data, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Shared links and exports

Share links contain model names and calculator settings in the URL fragment. Anyone receiving the link can read those values. They are not encrypted. CSV exports contain the selected statistics and model names; you decide where to save or send them.

Hosting

Cloudflare serves this site. Connecting to it necessarily sends network information such as your IP address and requested file address to the hosting provider. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its processing practices.

Advertising and privacy choices

Ad serving is currently disabled while Google reviews this website. Ordinary calculator visits do not load advertising scripts. The ad-placement preview is a local placeholder.

A separate consent preview loads Google’s consent and advertising software with ad requests paused. This preview does not request an ad, even if you accept. Loading Google’s software sends network information to Google and may allow Google to read existing cookies on its domains.

When advertising is activated, one Google AdSense display unit will appear below the calculator. Google Advertising Products is the configured advertising partner. Google may process cookies, device identifiers, IP address and ad-interaction information to select and measure ads. Personalised advertising uses additional consent choices. Sensou Calc includes no analytics SDK.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. We keep ad requests paused until a valid choice allows the necessary Google purposes; we do not rely on legitimate interest for our ad-loading decision. Where the consent API says these European rules do not apply, its regional result is used. A browser Global Privacy Control signal disables our ad requests.

Where available, use Privacy & cookie settings to reopen Google’s message and change or withdraw your choice. This immediately pauses our ad requests and removes any current ad. Declining advertising does not affect the calculator. No ads are loaded while the device is offline.

See how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google’s privacy policy for Google’s processing practices.

Project questions

Technical questions and reports can be submitted through the public project issue tracker. Issues are public; do not include private information.
